Import Rocksmith packs

Converts Rocksmith 2014 .psarc files into songs in your library, using Rocksmith 2014 DLC Builder. Song packs that hold many songs (such as songs.psarc) work too. Files that were already converted are skipped.

Drop .psarc files here, or click to choose them.

The files are copied, converted, then the copies are removed. Your originals are never changed.
